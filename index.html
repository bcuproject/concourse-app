Running the two-app demo: open the Customer app and the Kitchen app in two windows of the same browser on the same device (e.g. side by side on a laptop). Place an order in the customer app — it lands on the kitchen screen instantly. Press Ready — notify customer and the customer app gets the notification. Or leave the kitchen's autopilot on and orders go ready on their own after 2 minutes.

Tip: tap anywhere once first so the browser allows the notification sounds.

Concept prototype — not affiliated with the venue or club. The kiosk list comes from the venue's matchday food & drink page; prices, menus, queue times, adverts and the match itself are illustrative. Payments are simulated.
